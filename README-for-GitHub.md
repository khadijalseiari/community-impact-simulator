## About

Players improve one Abu Dhabi community by tapping areas that need help. Each choice activates a real ADPHC **Community Health** initiative and transforms that place in real time — the city becomes more active, better nourished, safer at work, greener and happier, while avoidable healthcare costs fall. Reach **100%** to become a *Community Health Champion*.

This is the **External · Firefly** edition: it adds luminous firefly ambiance that blooms at dusk and during the finale, a self-playing demo mode for unattended booths, an on-screen About panel, and a shareable-link-friendly setup.

## Initiatives featured

SEHHI Healthy Food · citymoov (gamified physical activity) · Active Abu Dhabi walking & cycling advocacy · Healthy Workplace & Cafeterias · Occupational Safety & Health and Heat Safety · Calories on Menu · School Nutrition · IFHAS Community Screening & Early Detection · Health Promotion · Community Activation (Mall Walks & Health Hub).

## How to run

- **Online:** open the GitHub Pages link above in any modern browser (Edge, Chrome, Safari, Firefox).
- **Offline / kiosk:** download `index.html`, double-click to open in a browser, press **F11** for full screen.
- **Touch displays:** fully touch-optimized; works on large interactive screens and tablets.

### Keyboard shortcuts
`Enter` start · `L` language (EN/AR) · `R` restart · `Esc` close panel · `D` demo mode · `F` fireflies · `F11` full screen.

## Technical notes

- **Single self-contained HTML file.** No dependencies, no build step, no server, no internet required at runtime.
- **No data collected.** Nothing is transmitted or stored; everything runs locally in the browser.
- **Scope:** Community Health initiatives only. No communicable-disease, vaccination or infection-prevention content.
- Indicator values are **illustrative**, designed to show the direction and relative impact of prevention — not published statistics or forecasts.
